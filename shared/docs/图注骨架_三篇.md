### Figure 4. ZP3 shows univariate survival association in CGGA-325 but not after molecular-subtype adjustment.
**Legend (EN):**
Kaplan–Meier overall-survival curves stratified by ZP3-high vs ZP3-low in CGGA-325 (left) and CGGA-693 (right). Univariate Cox: CGGA-325 HR=1.24 per unit (95% CI 1.14–1.34, p=1.2e-7; log-rank p=0.0008, median split); CGGA-693 HR=1.00 (p=0.47; log-rank p=0.12). After adjustment for age, grade, IDH, and 1p/19q, the CGGA-325 association was no longer significant (HR=1.09, 95% CI 0.99–1.21, p=0.070); CGGA-693 remained null (HR=1.00, p=0.70). Forest plot shows HRs with 95% CI. ZP3 survival association is therefore **not independent of molecular subtype** — consistent with ZP3 as a context/bystander marker whose apparent prognostic value is confounded by IDH status.

**数据/方法注记:**
- 来源：CGGA-693 + CGGA-325；`output/cgga_validation/cgga325_cox_freeze.py` + `cgga325_cox_results.csv`（2026-08-14 固化）
- 统计：单变量 Cox：CGGA-325 HR=1.236 (p=1.45e-7)，CGGA-693 HR=1.001 (p=0.467)；多变量 Cox（Age/Grade/IDH/1p19q）：CGGA-325 HR=1.094 (p=0.070)，CGGA-693 HR=1.001 (p=0.703)；Log-rank：CGGA-325 p=0.0008、CGGA-693 p=0.12
- 🔴 **2026-08-14 审计重大修正**：原图注称"multivariable Cox HR=1.236, ZP3 retained significance in CGGA-325, adjusted for age/grade/IDH/1p19q" —— **错误**：HR=1.236 是**单变量** Cox 结果；多变量调整后 p=0.070 **不显著**。CGGA-693 实测 p=0.47（原记 0.97 无来源）。叙事改为"单变量显著、调整分子亚型后消失"——与旁观者标志物假说一致，但**不得再称 ZP3 为独立预后因子**。
